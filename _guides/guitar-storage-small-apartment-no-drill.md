<p class="lede">The corner beside a desk can look just wide enough for a guitar until you pull out the chair. Hold your guitar where you plan to keep it and move the chair or open the nearest door. Check the widest part of the body as well as the tuning pegs. That quick fit check will tell you whether to use the floor, a desk edge or the closet.</p>

<h2 id="pick-a-guitar-spot">Where can the guitar stay between sessions?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#hercules-ezpack-gs200b">Beside your practice chair.</a> A folding stand supports the guitar on the floor and packs away when you move the instrument back to its case.</li>
  <li><a href="#daddario-guitar-dock">Against a desk during a playing break.</a> A clamp gives the neck a resting place while the floor carries the guitar's weight.</li>
  <li><a href="#original-closet-guitar-hanger">Inside a closet with a clear section of rod.</a> A hanger gets the guitar off the floor between sessions.</li>
</ul>
