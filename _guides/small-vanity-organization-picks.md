<h2 id="choose-a-getting-ready-zone">Choose one getting-ready zone</h2>

<p>First clear a working patch large enough to set down the items you use. Measure the remaining surface and any shelf above it. A compact organizer still needs access space: brushes extend above a caddy, a drawer opens forward, trays turn, and a mirror needs room to tilt. These are freestanding accessories, so they do not require drilling into a rental's walls.</p>

<h2 id="separate-everyday-cosmetics">1. Separate the cosmetics you use every day</h2>

<p>Choose a daily set before choosing a container. Tall tools need upright compartments, while small tubes and compacts benefit from a shallower space where they remain visible. Keep occasional-use supplies elsewhere if squeezing them into the same caddy would hide the items you reach for most.</p>

<h2 id="sort-accessories-upward">2. Sort small accessories upward</h2>

<p>A tiered tray is useful when several little dishes are taking over the surface. Give each level a simple category, such as today's jewelry, watch accessories, or hair clips. Leave each piece easy to retrieve; a shallow tray is not a substitute for hanging long chains separately.</p>

<h2 id="combine-mirror-and-catchall">3. Combine a mirror with a small catch-all</h2>

<h2 id="keep-the-surface-usable">Keep the surface usable</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Mock up the footprint with paper before ordering, then leave extra room for hands, drawer travel, and rotating parts.</li>
  <li>Reserve one clear working area. More storage is not helpful if it removes the last place to set something down.</li>
  <li>Measure the actual cosmetics and accessories, not just the furniture. A product's outside size does not establish compartment capacity.</li>
  <li>Keep liquids securely closed and accessories away from sink edges. Return seldom-used items to separate storage.</li>
  <li>Before buying, confirm the selected size, color, model, condition, seller, delivery terms, and current offer. None of these picks requires purchasing the other two.</li>
</ul>
