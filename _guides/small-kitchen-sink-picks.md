<h2 id="choose-sink-job">Choose the part of the routine that needs help</h2>

<p>Measure the space with the faucet, dish soap, and anything you cannot move still in place. Leave a working area for food preparation and a clear route for water to reach the sink. An empty organizer's footprint is only the starting point: dishes, handles, and brushes extend beyond it.</p>

<h2 id="wash-in-basin">1. Check whether a separate washing bowl helps</h2>

<p>A basin can separate a washing task from the sink itself, but it also reduces the space available around dishes. Check the largest plate or pan you regularly wash, the sink's rounded corners, and the height below the faucet. You should still be able to reach the plug comfortably.</p>

<h2 id="clear-drying-zone">2. Make the drying area temporary</h2>

<p>If counter space is more valuable after washing than during it, a rack-and-mat setup is worth considering. Lay out a paper rectangle before ordering, then place your usual plates and cups within that area. Keep the mat fully supported on a suitable counter, with room for items that project beyond the rack.</p>

<h2 id="store-cleaning-tools">3. Separate cleaning tools from the drying dishes</h2>

<p>Choose a tool-storage position you can reach without stretching over clean plates. Leave room to operate the faucet and remove the drip tray. If the only available ledge is narrow or sloped, measure it carefully rather than assuming a freestanding caddy will be suitable.</p>

<h2 id="plan-after-washing">Plan for the counter after washing, too</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Keep enough clear space to prepare food and move dishes without reaching across a crowded drying area.</li>
  <li>Check faucet movement, overhead clearance, sink corners, and the space needed to remove trays or plugs.</li>
  <li>Clean and dry the organizer and the surface beneath it according to their care instructions.</li>
  <li>Confirm the model, color, included pieces, seller, item condition, delivery terms, returns, and current availability before ordering.</li>
</ul>
