<p class="lede">A hair dryer can fit under the sink until you add the nozzle and try to close the door. With a flat iron, you need somewhere to rest the plates while they cool and enough drawer space for the handle and plug. Start with the tool you reach for most and measure it the way you put it away, attachments and all.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-spot">Where do you have room?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#spectrum-grid-87877">Inside the vanity door.</a> If a dryer, brush and cords take up most of a shelf, check the space the door swings through.</li>
  <li><a href="#yamazaki-rin-1333">On a narrow strip of counter.</a> If you put the dryer down every time you section your hair, measure the parts of the tool that would rest on the stand.</li>
  <li><a href="#polder-hot-sleeve">In a drawer or gym bag.</a> If you use one straightener, check its width and the length of the entire tool before choosing a sleeve.</li>
</ul>
