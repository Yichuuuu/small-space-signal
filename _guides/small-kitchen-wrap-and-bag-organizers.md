<p>Start by removing empty cartons and consolidating duplicates. Keep a week's actual selection of bags and wraps together before measuring. If you use only one bag size and one roll, a divided tray you already own may be enough. These are alternatives or separate stations, not a three-piece kit that we claim fits one drawer.</p>

<p>Measure inside the drawer, including its narrowest opening and clearance beneath any rail above it. For a cabinet, account for hinges, door closure, and the space your hand needs to remove a box. A product's outside dimensions do not describe the room required to load, use, or carry it.</p>

<h2 id="make-one-change-first">Make one change first</h2>

<p>Choose the category that currently causes the most friction. Mark its proposed footprint with a paper template, check the door or drawer movement, and only then decide whether a dedicated organizer earns its space. Keep clean food-contact supplies separate from cleaning chemicals and leave the counter clear when food preparation is finished.</p>
