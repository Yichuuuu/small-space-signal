<p class="lede">Fasten your longest necklace, loop it over a finger and measure down to the bottom of the pendant. That hanging length tells you more about the stand you need than the chain length on its tag. Next, think about where you put jewelry on. A pair of studs by the mirror, a necklace chosen with an outfit and a few pieces packed for a weekend each need a different kind of space.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-jewelry-spot">Where will you reach for your jewelry?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#yamazaki-tosca-accessory-tree">On the dresser by the mirror.</a> Keep a small daily rotation on branches, with a tray beneath for rings and studs.</li>
  <li><a href="#bagsmart-hanging-organizer">Inside the clothes closet.</a> Give necklaces one face of a hanging organizer and smaller pieces their own visible pockets.</li>
  <li><a href="#benevolence-velvet-case">In a drawer you also pack from.</a> A zippered case can move a few favorite pieces straight into your overnight bag.</li>
</ul>
