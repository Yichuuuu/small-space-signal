<p class="lede">If you knit or crochet on the sofa, what do you have to pick up before someone else can sit down? For a scarf it might be one ball that keeps rolling under the couch. For a striped blanket it might be several skeins and the growing blanket itself. Start with the part that needs a home, then measure the spot where you want to put it.</p>

<h2 id="where-do-your-supplies-go">Where do your supplies go between rows?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#yarn-valet-bowl">On the end table.</a> Give the ball you are working from a six-inch circle beside your chair.</li>
  <li><a href="#artbin-yarn-drum">On a shelf between sessions.</a> Carry the yarn for a multicolor project back to the sofa in one trip.</li>
  <li><a href="#artbin-hook-needle-folio">In a shallow drawer.</a> Give loose hooks and spare needles their own slots so you can find the size you need.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="putting-a-project-away">Make it easy to pick up the next row</h2>

<p>Write the hook or needle size, last completed row and next color on a slip of paper. Tuck it into the folded work with the yarn band. When two projects share a shelf, that little note lets you return a spare hook to its case and still know which one to take out next time.</p>
