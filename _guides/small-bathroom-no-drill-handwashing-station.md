<p>Clear the sink surround first. Keep a working original soap pump or an existing towel rail if it already fits. Measure usable flat counter space rather than the sink's outside dimensions, then check the faucet handle and medicine-cabinet door through their full movement. On the floor, include the bathroom door and cabinet doors in your clearance check.</p>

<p>A countertop towel stand is a poor choice for a pedestal sink with almost no ledge. It can make sense on a vanity with an unused corner, provided the towel does not drape into the basin or across the soap pump. Think of the products below as separate fixes, not a requirement to buy all three.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-sink-easy-to-clean">Keep the sink easy to clean</h2>

<p>Test the arrangement before buying a full set: mark the proposed footprints on paper, open every nearby door, and move your hands through the usual washing routine. A towel should not brush the pump, the pump should not block the faucet, and the bin should not narrow your standing space. Leave gaps that let you lift each item and wipe beneath it.</p>
