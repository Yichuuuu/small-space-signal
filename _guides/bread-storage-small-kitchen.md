<p class="lede">Before giving up a patch of counter to a bread box, take your usual loaf to the pantry. Try it upright on a shelf and check whether you can reach the first slice. If the shelf is too low, look at the counter space below a cabinet. If you freeze most of the loaf anyway, measure the drawer with the other food still in it.</p>

  <h3 id="rezip-bread-bag">(re)zip roll-top bread and bagel freezer bag</h3>
  <p>If you freeze half a loaf after shopping, slice that portion first so you can take out what you need. This clear <strong>39-cup</strong> bag measures <strong>8 × 7 × 16 inches</strong>. Leave enough empty material above the bread to press out excess air, seal the top and roll it down tightly three times.
