<p>These are separate purchases, not a bundled repair kit. Keep a furniture-supplied tool if it works, and use the tool and tightening method specified by the furniture manufacturer. A compact substitute is not automatically appropriate for every fastener or joint.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-job-within-rental-boundaries">Keep the job within rental boundaries</h2>

<p>Use this guide for suitable small tasks on your own furniture or loose project parts, within your skills and the item's instructions. It is not a guide to electrical, gas, plumbing, structural, fire-safety, or security-system repairs. Report those issues to the responsible person. Do not use these tools on energized equipment or infer electrical protection from a handle's appearance.</p>

<p>A dining table is not automatically a workbench. Use a stable, appropriate work surface and finish-compatible protection; keep children and pets out of the work area. Required furniture anchoring and other safety steps still apply. If a required installation needs permission, obtain it instead of skipping the safety step.</p>

<h2 id="grip-small-parts">1. Grip small parts without storing full-size pliers</h2>

It is a poor default for decorative hardware or a job that needs a specified wrench or controlled tightening torque.</p>

<h2 id="check-a-local-surface">2. Check a local surface without storing a long level</h2>

<p>A pocket level suits a quick orientation check on a small furniture surface. It can help distinguish a tilted surface from an impression created by nearby trim or an uneven visual reference. It cannot establish whether an entire long shelf is straight, whether furniture is stable, or whether a mounting system is safe.</p>

<h2 id="hold-a-light-project-temporarily">3. Hold a light project temporarily</h2>

<p>The BESSEY clamp suits small, light-duty holding tasks where the jaws can contact appropriate surfaces: for example, keeping loose project pieces aligned during a permitted dry fit. Before buying, measure both the total thickness to be gripped and how far the pressure point sits from the edge. Those are different measurements.</p>

<h2 id="give-each-tool-a-storage-place">Give each tool a storage place</h2>

<p>Measure the drawer's usable depth and clearance under its frame, then leave room to lift each tool out. Keep gripping teeth and clamp edges away from delicate stored items, and keep the level's contact faces clean. An existing tray or pouch may be enough; none of the three listings is presented here as a complete storage kit.</p>
