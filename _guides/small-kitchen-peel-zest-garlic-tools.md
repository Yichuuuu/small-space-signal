<h2 id="plan-one-accessible-prep-drawer">Plan one accessible prep drawer</h2>

<p>Start with a clear work surface, a stable cutting board, and a bowl for ingredients or scraps. Measure the usable length, width, and height inside your drawer, including any organizer already there. Allow room to grasp a handle without reaching across a blade. None of these products includes a drawer tray, and we have not tested their combined fit.</p>

<p>All three work without drilling, wiring, or permanent mounting, so they can move with you between rentals. That does not make every tool necessary. If you rarely zest citrus, or your knife already handles the small amount of garlic you use, leaving a space empty can be the better storage decision.</p>

<h2 id="peel-with-an-inline-hand-tool">1. Peel produce with an inline hand tool</h2>

<p>An inline peeler is worth considering for someone who regularly prepares potatoes, carrots, or apples and prefers its handle position to a Y-shaped peeler. Keep it near the board rather than buried beneath serving utensils. The choice is about your usual grip and prep routine, not a claim that one shape works best for everyone.</p>

<h2 id="give-the-zester-a-long-storage-lane">2. Give a narrow zester a long storage lane</h2>

<p>A rasp-style zester can work over a bowl for small amounts of citrus zest or a hard-cheese topping. Its narrow shape avoids a standing grater box, but the handle and blade still need length in the drawer. This pick suits cooks who frequently use fine grated finishes, not someone primarily preparing large amounts of shredded vegetables.</p>

<h2 id="put-the-tools-away-without-a-pile">Put the tools away without a pile</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Choose a long compartment for the covered zester and separate spaces for the peeler and rocker. Avoid a stack that hides cutting surfaces.</li>
  <li>Check that handles are accessible and the drawer closes without contact. A quoted tool length does not include the extra room your fingers need.</li>
  <li>Clean and dry tools before storing them. Keep sharp tools out of children's reach and do not leave them concealed in washing-up water.</li>
  <li>Buy for a repeated task, not to complete this list. These tools are not a matched storage kit, and no combined footprint is promised.</li>
</ul>
