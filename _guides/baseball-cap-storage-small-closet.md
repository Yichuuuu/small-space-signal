<p class="lede">Take the cap you wear most often out of the pile. Does it have an opening above an adjustable back strap, or a closed, fitted back? That small detail determines whether it can hang on a door clip. For shelf storage, use the cap with the widest brim to check the space. The hat can stick out well beyond the rack that holds it.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-cap-storage-spot">Which part of the closet can you use?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#perfect-curve-caprack">The back of a hinged door.</a> Keep adjustable caps in a vertical row where you can see the fronts.</li>
  <li><a href="#caruuyi-shelf-stands">A shelf at eye level.</a> Stand caps in slots so you can lift one out by its brim.</li>
  <li><a href="#procase-cap-carrier">A shelf for occasional wear or an overnight bag.</a> Pack a small group of caps together inside a rigid shell.</li>
</ul>
