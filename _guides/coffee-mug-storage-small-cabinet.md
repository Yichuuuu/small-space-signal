<p class="lede">Put your usual coffee mug at the front of the cabinet with its handle facing the way you grab it. Now close the door. That little space beside the handle is easy to overlook when measuring a shelf. If the shelf pins are adjustable, try moving the shelf one hole and check the dishes on the level above before buying an organizer.</p>

<h2 id="choose-by-shelf-space">Where is the unused space?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#elypro-mug-stackers">Above pairs of mugs.</a> An adjustable disc lets two mugs share one spot on the shelf.</li>
  <li><a href="#wenko-klea-hanger">Below a deep shelf.</a> A slide-on hanger can use a narrow strip beside a row of plates.</li>
  <li><a href="#madesmart-stacking-shelf">Above short cups you reach for separately.</a> A freestanding riser gives each level its own shelf, including space for cups without handles.</li>
</ul>
