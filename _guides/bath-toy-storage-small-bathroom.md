<p class="lede">When a child's bath and an adult's shower share the same tub, the awkward part is where to put the toys while they drip. A hanging scoop and a basket across the rim let water drain back into the tub. A bin with a catch tray lets you clear the tub for the next person. Start by checking which of those spots you actually have.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-drying-spot">Which drying spot fits your bathroom?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#munchkin-super-scoop">A smooth patch of wall above the tub.</a> Hang a mesh scoop here if the shower curtain can still slide past it.</li>
  <li><a href="#kidco-bath-basket">Two clear tub rims facing each other.</a> A slotted basket lets you see the toys in one layer and drains straight into the bath.</li>
  <li><a href="#ubbi-drying-bin">A shelf or counter beside the tub.</a> A freestanding bin works with textured walls and gives the drips somewhere to go.</li>
</ul>
