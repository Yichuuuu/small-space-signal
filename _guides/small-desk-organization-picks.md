<h2 id="lift-the-laptop">1. Give the laptop a defined place</h2>

<p>Mark out the stand's base on the desk, then allow for the laptop above it, its open screen, and any side-mounted plugs. Check the space left for a notebook and mouse. On a shallow desk, moving a laptop upward can still leave too little room in front of it.</p>

<h2 id="bundle-spare-length">2. Gather spare cable length without making it permanent</h2>

<p>Separate the lead that travels with your laptop from cables that stay at the desk. Leave enough slack to move each device normally, then gather only the excess. A bundle should open easily when you replace a charger or remove one cable.</p>

<h2 id="anchor-a-charging-lead">3. Keep a frequently used lead within reach</h2>

<p>Choose a spot you can reach while seated, away from the mouse path and drawer openings. Test the cable's route before attaching a clip. Leave a small service loop so unplugging the device does not pull directly on the mount.</p>

<h2 id="test-your-layout">Test the whole layout before ordering</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Measure usable desk space, including laptop overhang, plug clearance, and mouse movement.</li>
  <li>Open drawers and move the chair through its normal range. Nothing should pull on a cable.</li>
  <li>Keep frequently disconnected cables separate from permanent bundles.</li>
  <li>Match the model, size, and pack quantity on Amazon, then review the current seller, price, delivery terms, returns, and availability.</li>
</ul>
