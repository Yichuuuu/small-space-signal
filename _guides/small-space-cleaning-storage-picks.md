<h2 id="choose-cleaning-job">Choose the item that is hardest to put away</h2>

<p>Start with the storage route, not just the empty space. A drawer has to close around the tool, a hanging broom needs room for its head, and a loaded caddy must pass both cabinet hinges and plumbing. Keep daily-use items reachable without blocking a doorway or putting weight on pipes.</p>

<h2 id="store-a-small-brush">1. Keep a small brush and dustpan together</h2>

<p>For crumbs or a small craft-area cleanup, an accessible hand brush may be more convenient than bringing out a full-size broom. Choose its storage spot first. Avoid burying the set under other tools, and keep a brush used for dirty floor tasks separate from food-preparation surfaces.</p>

<h2 id="hang-compatible-handles">2. Hang a compatible broom without drilling</h2>

<p>Measure the handle where the gripper will hold it, rather than the wider grip at the top. Check the tool's total weight and the space its head occupies. For a rental, confirm that adhesive mounting is permitted and that the surface meets the product instructions; a no-drill product is not a guarantee against finish damage.</p>

<h2 id="roll-supplies-forward">3. Bring a small group of bottles out together</h2>

<p>Measure the clear cabinet floor around the drain, valves, hoses, and hinges. Then check the height of your tallest bottle and the space needed to tilt and lift the caddy. A rolling organizer will not solve a blocked exit or a high cabinet lip by itself.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-return-route-clear">Make putting things away part of the setup</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Choose storage that still works when the tool is wet, bulky, or loaded; dry tools according to their care instructions before enclosed storage.</li>
  <li>Keep cleaners in their original labeled containers, follow their storage directions, and keep them away from children and pets. A convenient caddy is not a safety lock.</li>
  <li>Do not attach organizers to plumbing or block access to valves, doors, or walkways.</li>
  <li>For adhesive products, read the exact pack's instructions before mounting and check the rental's rules.</li>
  <li>Confirm the model, pack count, seller, item condition, current availability, delivery terms, and returns before ordering.</li>
</ul>
