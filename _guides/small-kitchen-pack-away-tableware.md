<h2 id="measure-the-empty-storage-space">Measure the empty storage space</h2>

<p>Identify the actual bottleneck before buying anything. Is a mug too tall for a shallow drawer, a spare bowl occupying a whole shelf, or a fork disappearing into a shared cutlery tray? Collapsing changes height, not the footprint of the rim. Check the drawer opening and usable interior space, including any organizer already inside it.</p>

<h2 id="keep-a-fork-and-spoon-together">3. Keep a fork and spoon together</h2>

<p>GoBites Duo addresses organization more than cupboard volume. It can keep a personal fork and spoon together in a small drawer or an existing utensil pouch. It is useful for a shared kitchen or desk lunch routine where a separate pair has a purpose. An ordinary fork and spoon you already own may work just as well if you can give them a consistent home.</p>

<h2 id="make-the-reset-easy">Make the reset easy</h2>

<p>Store these items only after emptying, cleaning, and drying them. Sea to Summit's <a href="https://seatosummit.com/pages/warnings">product-specific warnings and cleaning instructions</a> call for complete drying and avoiding heavy-duty scouring pads on the silicone. Keep the fold lines accessible during cleaning. A compact stored shape is only useful if returning the item to its place is easy enough to do after each use.</p>
