<h2 id="carry-the-load">1. Use a basket that becomes shallow between loads</h2>

<p>Measure the shelf or gap where an empty basket will live. A collapsible basket can become much thinner without becoming shorter or narrower. Also check that you can carry it through your route to the washer without the handles or contents catching on a door.</p>

<h2 id="dry-over-rails">2. Allow enough room around an upright drying rack</h2>

<p>Mark the open footprint on the floor, then add the space taken up by hanging fabric. Choose a level location away from the walking route and heaters, with suitable protection for any drips. Leave enough room to reach the rails without moving wet clothes out of the way.</p>

<h2 id="support-flat-drying">3. Give flat-dry garments a supported surface</h2>

<p>If a care label calls for flat drying, first check whether the garment can lie in its intended shape within the available area. Keep fabric from hanging unsupported over an edge. A mesh surface is useful only when the support below it is stable and the surrounding area remains accessible.</p>

<h2 id="check-the-whole-routine">Check the whole laundry routine</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Match the drying method to the garment's care label, not just the space available.</li>
  <li>Leave clearance for fabric, carrying handles, loading, and passing through the room.</li>
  <li>Choose a location where damp air can disperse and occasional drips will not damage the surface.</li>
  <li>Confirm the exact model, size, pack quantity, current seller, delivery terms, returns, and availability on Amazon before ordering.</li>
</ul>
