<h2 id="test-your-counter">Try the fit before choosing</h2>

<p>Set your cutting board and largest mixing bowl where you usually work. Before you start cooking, prop the open book securely at the proposed spot. Read the ingredient list, reach into the bowl, and open the cupboard above it. A placement works when you can do all three without moving the recipe. On a shared dining table, check the storage spot too. The Booklift can fold beside the binder, while the Tosca needs shelf space for its standing frame.</p>

<p>If dough takes up most of the table, plan the rolling area first and put the recipe just outside its edge. See our <a href="{{ '/articles/small-kitchen-pack-away-pastry-tools.html' | relative_url }}">guide to a pastry setup you can put away</a>.</p>
