<p class="lede">Roll a 68-by-24-inch yoga mat from a short end and you have a roll about 24 inches long. Measure your own roll from end to end and across its widest point. Try it on a shelf with the closet door closed. If it stays put and is easy to reach, that shelf is all you need.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-mat-storage-spot">Where do you have room for the mat?</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#manduka-commuter">On a shelf beside your gym bag.</a> Leave a carrier around the roll if you regularly take the mat to class.</li>
  <li><a href="#yamazaki-door-hanger">Behind a closet door.</a> Weigh your mat and check the door thickness before choosing a hanger.</li>
  <li><a href="#gaiam-foldable-mat">In a drawer too short for a roll.</a> If you are choosing a new mat, a scored folding model stores flat.</li>
</ul>
