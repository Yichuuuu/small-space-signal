<p class="lede">Glasses compete with phones, books and water glasses on a small nightstand. Sunglasses can spread across a dresser just as quickly. Choose storage around how often you reach for each pair: an open holder for everyday use, a vertical rack for a rotation, or a closed case for pairs you put away. You do not need all three.</p>

<h2 id="measure-glasses-and-space">Measure the glasses as well as the space</h2>

<p>Fold your largest frames and measure their width, depth and height. Outside organizer dimensions describe the space it occupies, not its usable interior. Curved sports frames and large lenses need particular attention. On a shelf, leave room above a holder to lift glasses out; for a case, allow room to unzip it and open the lid. On a narrow bedside table, keep the storage spot away from the edge and your drinking glass.</p>
