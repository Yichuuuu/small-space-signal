<p class="lede">In a small kitchen, a paper towel roll can occupy the same patch of counter you need for chopping or setting down groceries. Start by choosing where the roll belongs: on a movable stand, beside the fridge or on a cabinet door. These three holders offer different ways to use the kitchen you already have.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-location">Choose a location before a holder</h2>

<ul>
  <li><a href="#oxo-simplytear">OXO SimplyTear:</a> for a small, clear counter area when you want to move the roll between the sink and table.</li>
  <li><a href="#yamazaki-tosca-magnetic">Yamazaki Tosca magnetic hanger:</a> for an accessible fridge surface that accepts magnets, leaving the counter free.</li>
  <li><a href="#umbra-squire-cabinet">Umbra Squire Multi-Use:</a> for a cabinet door with room for hanging hooks and a loaded roll.</li>
</ul>

<p>Choose one holder for your main working area. Moving a roll off the counter is useful only if it remains easy to reach without opening a door into your legs or stretching across the cooking area. Leave the space where you prepare food clear enough to work.</p>

<h2 id="measure-the-loaded-roll">Measure the loaded roll and the refill space</h2>

<p>Check the length and diameter of a fresh roll from the brand you normally buy. The empty holder's dimensions do not describe the full space needed by a loaded roll. Allow room to grip the paper and replace the roll without moving nearby appliances.</p>

<p>For a counter stand, include clearance above the spindle. For a magnetic holder, test the exact fridge panel with an ordinary magnet first; a metallic-looking finish does not establish compatibility. For a cabinet hanger, measure the door's top edge, surrounding trim and closing gap. Also check inside clearance if the roll will face into the cabinet.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-new-space-useful">Keep the newly cleared space useful</h2>

<p>Try the intended position through an ordinary kitchen routine: open the fridge, open the cabinet, reach for a towel and replace the roll. A holder that clears the counter but interferes with those actions needs a different location. Keep one active roll within reach and store replacements in a dry cupboard rather than filling the newly cleared surface.</p>
