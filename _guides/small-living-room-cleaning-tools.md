<h2 id="choose-the-job-before-the-tool">Choose the job before the tool</h2>

<p>Pick a storage location before buying: an existing cleaning caddy, a utility shelf, or a drawer away from food and clean textiles. Leave room to lift tools out without unpacking everything. Wet cloths need somewhere to dry before they return to that storage spot. None of these products requires a wall-mounted installation, but each still needs a care routine.</p>

<h2 id="lift-hair-from-upholstery">1. Lift loose hair from upholstery</h2>

<p>If the sofa needs frequent hair or lint touch-ups, a reusable brush may be more convenient to keep nearby than a supply of adhesive sheets. It is a different job from removing a spill or cleaning beneath the cushions. Follow the upholstery maker's care guidance and try a hidden area first, especially on a delicate or loosely woven fabric.</p>

<h2 id="separate-glass-cleaning-and-polishing">2. Separate glass cleaning from polishing</h2>

<p>For a mirror or glass tabletop, a washable two-cloth kit makes the wet and dry steps distinct. Use it only on surfaces whose care instructions permit this method. A glass-cleaning label is not permission to use the same cloth on every coated display, lens, or specialty finish.</p>

<h2 id="reach-dust-around-keys">3. Reach loose dust around keyboard keys</h2>

<p>A living-room keyboard or shared work surface can benefit from a dedicated detail tool. Use gentle, dry cleaning only where the device maker permits it. Turn off and unplug equipment first; avoid forcing a tip into connectors, removing components, or treating a household brush as a repair tool.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-kit-small-and-ready">Keep the kit small and ready</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Buy for a recurring task, not to fill every space in a caddy.</li>
  <li>Separate upholstery debris, glass cloths, and electronics tools rather than sharing a dirty cleaning surface between them.</li>
  <li>Empty the furniture brush base, protect the electronics brush ends, and dry cloths before storage.</li>
  <li>Measure the actual brush-and-base or capped-tool footprint before committing to fitted drawer compartments. A verified complete storage measurement for the furniture brush was not available in the primary references reviewed.</li>
