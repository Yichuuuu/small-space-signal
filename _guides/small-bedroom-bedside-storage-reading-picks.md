<p class="lede">A narrow space beside the bed does not always need another piece of furniture. A small shelf can provide a landing spot, a hanging pocket can keep a book off the floor, and a clip-on light can leave an existing tabletop free. These three picks solve different problems. Start with the one task your current setup cannot handle; they are not a bundle you need to buy together.</p>

<h2 id="check-the-bed-before-buying">Check the bed, not just the gap beside it</h2>

<p>First list what you actually reach for at night: perhaps one book, glasses in a case, and a remote. Then sit and stand beside the bed and check the route to the door. A product without legs still takes up space at mattress height. It can also obstruct a storage drawer, bedding, or the spot where you swing your legs out of bed.</p>

<h2 id="add-a-small-landing-surface">1. Add a small landing surface</h2>

<p>A shelf is the more useful choice if the missing function is somewhere to set a small item down. Before buying, cut a paper rectangle to the tray dimensions and hold it at the proposed position. Check it while getting into bed, opening nearby drawers, and making the bed. Do not assume a shelf that fits the mattress also leaves a comfortable walkway.</p>

<h2 id="move-loose-items-into-a-pocket">2. Move loose items into a pocket</h2>

<p>If you do not need a flat surface, a pocket can keep a paperback and a remote together without introducing another floor-standing unit. Put glasses in their case before storing them with other objects. Keep the contents modest: the aim is to find a few things easily, not to turn the bedside into a deep archive of books and cables.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-light-on-the-book">3. Keep the reading light on the book</h2>

<p>A book light is an alternative to using some of your newly cleared surface for a lamp base. It follows the book when you change position, but it is not a substitute for lighting the route out of the room. Consider where it will be charged and stored before adding another rechargeable item to an already crowded bedside.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-bedside-easy-to-use">Keep the bedside easy to use</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Choose a shelf or pocket around your actual belongings and bed construction. Buying both may add clutter without solving another problem.</li>
  <li>Keep cables out of the walking route and away from hinges, drawers, and moving bed mechanisms. Do not trap charging equipment under the mattress or pillows.</li>
  <li>Recheck reach and stability with the bed made. A thick duvet can cover an opening or catch on a projecting shelf.</li>
  <li>Confirm the selected model, color, included accessories, seller, and delivery terms on Amazon before ordering. Listing fields and available offers can change.</li>
</ul>
