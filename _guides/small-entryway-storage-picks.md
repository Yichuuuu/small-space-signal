<h2 id="choose-your-zone">Choose a storage zone outside the walking route</h2>

<p>Open the door fully and walk in carrying your usual bag. Any space needed for that movement is not spare storage space. Clear out seasonal shoes and rarely used accessories before deciding which of these products, if any, would help.</p>

<h2 id="hang-light-accessories">1. Hang light accessories on a suitable interior door</h2>

<p>An entry closet door can be a useful location, provided it still opens, closes, and latches normally. Check its full swing with a bag held at the proposed hanging position. Avoid adding hardware to a fire-rated door or a door with a closer unless the installation is explicitly approved.</p>

<h2 id="park-keys">2. Give keys a shallow, reachable landing place</h2>

<p>Choose a position you can reach with one hand while keeping the doorway clear. A small tray is most useful when it stays limited to a few daily essentials. If a normal household magnet does not hold securely to the intended surface, choose a different mounting approach.</p>

<h2 id="line-up-shoes">3. Keep everyday shoes on a low, adjustable rack</h2>

<p>Measure your largest regular shoes from heel to toe and place them in the proposed location before ordering. The rack's frame depth is not the loaded footprint: toes or heels may extend farther into the hallway. Keep the door sweep and a comfortable walking route clear.</p>
