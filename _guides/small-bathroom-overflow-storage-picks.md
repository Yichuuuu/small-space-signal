<h2 id="measure-the-working-space">Measure the working space, not just the empty spot</h2>

<p>Make a quick paper footprint before ordering. Open the vanity door, pull out the item you intend to store, and leave room for cleaning around it. A towel needs space to hang; a styling tool extends above its holder; a spare roll needs to lift over a post. None of these recommended setups requires drilling, but each has a different fit limitation.</p>

<h2 id="give-one-hand-towel-a-place">1. Give one hand towel a place on the vanity door</h2>

<p>A short cabinet-door bar makes sense when the sink edge is doing duty as a towel rack. It is especially relevant in a rental where adding wall hardware is not an option. This is a small hand-towel solution, not a substitute for the space needed to spread out a full bath towel.</p>

<h2 id="separate-tools-from-cords">2. Separate styling tools from their cords</h2>

<p>A dedicated holder can be useful if you actually use several styling tools and a shallow drawer keeps snagging their cables. First check whether the dryer body and straightener will fit the openings. For someone with just one small dryer, keeping its existing drawer organized may use less space than adding an upright station.</p>

<h2 id="keep-a-small-roll-reserve">3. Keep a small reserve of standard toilet rolls</h2>

<p>If the bulk pack lives in another room, a narrow spare-roll stand can keep a few replacements nearby. It is not a reason to squeeze more supplies into an already crowded toilet area. Leave the floor around the toilet accessible, and choose a dry location where the rolls will not be knocked when you reach for cleaning supplies.</p>

<h2 id="leave-space-for-the-routine">Leave space for the bathroom routine</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Try the intended location with the door open and closed. Brackets, towel folds, cords, and your hands all need room beyond the organizer itself.</li>
  <li>Keep damp towels, electrical tools, and spare paper in separate suitable spots. A compact layout should not put everything beside the sink.</li>
  <li>Use the outline test before buying: mark the footprint with a sheet of paper and walk through your normal morning routine.</li>
  <li>Confirm the selected model, finish, dimensions, seller, and delivery details on Amazon. A different color or larger size is not automatically the same fit.</li>
</ul>
