<p class="lede">A portable caddy gives a group of supplies one place to live and one handle to carry. That can help when cleaning products share a kitchen cabinet, bathroom essentials move between rooms, or a dining table doubles as a craft desk. Choose the contents and their parking spot first: a useful caddy must fit the cabinet opening, clear the plumbing, and come out without unpacking it.</p>

<h2 id="measure-the-storage-route">Measure the route into the cabinet</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Make the kit first.</strong> Put the supplies you actually use in a group. Include caps, trigger sprayers, and brushes; these often extend beyond the bottle bases. Keep backup stock separate if it would make the everyday kit awkward to carry.</li>
  <li><strong>Check the narrowest opening.</strong> Measure between hinges and around any center door stile. A wide shelf behind a narrow door does not guarantee that a loaded caddy will pass through.</li>
  <li><strong>Try a paper footprint.</strong> Cut a paper rectangle to the listed length and width. Place it on the shelf, then move it through the opening. Add clearance rather than planning a friction fit against pipes or cabinet sides.</li>
  <li><strong>Check the loaded height.</strong> Use the taller of the caddy and its contents, then leave room to grip and lift the handle. A shelf above the handle can make an otherwise suitable footprint inconvenient.</li>
</ol>

<p>If you already have a basket that passes these checks, try it for a week before replacing it. The useful change may be reducing the contents and assigning a storage spot.</p>

<h2 id="give-the-caddy-a-home">Give the caddy one job and one parking spot</h2>

<p>Keep a cleaning kit separate from toiletries and craft materials, and follow each cleaning product's storage instructions. Return tools dry and keep labels visible. A portable caddy is open storage; it does not provide a locked enclosure for household chemicals.</p>

<p>For a crowded under-sink cabinet, decide whether you want to carry the whole kit to another room or simply bring the back row forward. Our <a href="{{ '/articles/small-space-cleaning-storage-picks.html' | relative_url }}">cleaning storage guide</a> includes a rolling under-sink option and ways to store long-handled tools. If the supplies themselves take up too much space, compare the <a href="{{ '/articles/small-space-manual-cleaning-tools.html' | relative_url }}">manual cleaning tools guide</a> before adding another container.</p>

<p>Leave the chosen parking spot open after the caddy comes out. That makes returning the kit a single action and keeps an empty shelf from becoming another pile of loose supplies.</p>
