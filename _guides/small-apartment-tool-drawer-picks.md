<h2 id="start-with-the-job-and-the-drawer">Start with the job, then choose the drawer</h2>

<p>List two or three real tasks before adding tools: measuring a cupboard opening, adjusting a screw on your own drawer handle, or assembling a small desk using its instructions. Keep any supplied furniture key if it works. An existing pouch or divided tray can keep the tools and a labeled bag of spare hardware together; none of the products below includes that storage container.</p>

<h2 id="measure-before-ordering-storage">1. Measure the usable space before ordering storage</h2>

<p>For a shelf bin, measure the clear opening and the usable interior separately. Door hinges, drawer slides, shelf lips, and handles can reduce the space through which an organizer must pass. Write down width, depth, and height with the units, then allow the access room needed to take the item out. A nominal furniture width is not an internal storage dimension.</p>

<h2 id="match-the-driver-to-the-fastener">2. Keep a short multi-bit driver for reachable fasteners</h2>

<p>A stubby driver can be useful when a longer handle collides with a nearby shelf or side panel. The tradeoff is reach: it may not reach a deeply recessed screw, and your hand still needs turning space. Inspect the fastener head and the approach angle before deciding that a shorter tool solves the problem.</p>

<h2 id="keep-metric-hex-keys-together">3. Keep frequently needed metric hex keys in one handle</h2>

<p>A folding set can stop loose hex keys from spreading through a utility drawer. It makes sense when your furniture actually uses the included metric sizes and there is room for the handle to turn. For a fastener deep inside a frame, a separate long key may still be the better fit.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-kit-small-and-specific">Keep the kit small and specific</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Store tools dry, with the tape retracted and hex keys folded. Keep the kit and loose bits away from children.</li>
  <li>Label spare hardware by furniture item. Keep assembly instructions available instead of assuming one tightening method suits every fitting.</li>
  <li>Measure your pouch or drawer before buying; the combined fit of these three products has not been tested.</li>
  <li>Check model, included pieces, seller, condition, and current delivery and return terms on Amazon. Keep tools you already own if they cover the same jobs.</li>
</ul>
