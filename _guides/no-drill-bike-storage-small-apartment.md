<p class="lede">Stand beside your bike in the spot where you want to park it. Open the nearest door and walk past the handlebar carrying your usual bag. If the bike fits, a roll-in stand makes daily departures easy. If a wheel sticks into the doorway, storing the bike upright may give you that stretch of floor back. For two bikes, the deciding measurement may be how high you can comfortably lift the upper one.</p>

<h2 id="measure-your-exit">Measure the way back out</h2>

<p>Mark the outer pedal and handlebar ends on the floor with slips of paper. Then move the bike out as you would on a weekday morning. A corner can fit a stationary bike and still leave too little room to turn it toward the door. If a shoe rack or a row of coats occupies that turning space, our <a href="{{ '/articles/no-drill-entryway.html' | relative_url }}">no-drill entryway guide</a> can help you rearrange the rest of the doorway.</p>
