<h2 id="contain-washable-delicates">1. Contain a washable garment without another rigid bin</h2>

<p>A mesh bag can give a larger washable item a defined place during a load without adding another basket to the laundry corner. It is useful when a garment needs separate containment, not as a way to turn every delicate into a machine-washable item. Check the clothing label first, particularly for knits that require hand washing or dry cleaning.</p>

<h2 id="hang-a-few-small-items">2. Hang a few small items from a support you already have</h2>

<p>Three individual hanging clips are useful when you need a place for socks or another small washable item, but do not want to unfold a full rack. Place drips over a suitable area and leave airflow around the fabric. This is not a flat-drying solution for heavy wet knits, and it is not a way to make an overloaded shower rod stronger.</p>

<h2 id="unfold-an-ironing-surface">3. Unfold an ironing surface on a suitable table</h2>

<p>A tabletop board can suit occasional ironing when there is no convenient home for a standing board. The tradeoff is that the table becomes part of the setup: it needs enough stable support, an appropriate working height, and a surface suitable for the ironing task. A folded board may fit in a cupboard even when the open board would dominate a small dining table.</p>

<h2 id="keep-the-laundry-corner-usable">Keep the laundry corner usable between loads</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Choose storage for the dry, empty item as well as a place to use it. Do not close damp accessories into a cupboard.</li>
  <li>Keep hanging laundry clear of walkways, doors, and fixtures you still need to use. A compact clip does not reduce the size of the garment below it.</li>
  <li>Follow garment labels and appliance instructions. A wash bag, clip, or board does not make an unsuitable washing, drying, or ironing method appropriate.</li>
  <li>Confirm the exact model, color, pack count, seller, and delivery details on Amazon. These are separate helpers, not a complete laundry system or a tested bundle.</li>
</ul>
