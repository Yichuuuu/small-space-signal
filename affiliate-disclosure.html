Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

How commercial links support this publication and how they are identified.

Required Amazon disclosure:
As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Small Space Signal may include affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and make an eligible purchase, this site may receive a commission. You do not pay a higher price because of that commission.

How links are labeled

Articles containing affiliate links will include a clear disclosure near the relevant recommendation or link. Labels may include “affiliate link,” “paid link,” or “#CommissionsEarned.” Readers should not have to search for the disclosure.

Editorial independence

Compensation does not guarantee coverage or a favorable conclusion. Selection criteria are developed around reader needs such as fit, maintenance, noise, durability, and rental risk. A product may be excluded when it fails those criteria even if an affiliate relationship is available.

Small Space Signal does not currently accept payment for positive reviews. If that changes, the sponsorship will be disclosed clearly in the relevant content.

Prices and availability

Retail prices and availability change frequently. Unless current information is supplied through an approved retailer tool, this site avoids presenting a price as guaranteed. Readers should verify the final price, seller, delivery date, and return terms on the retailer’s page before purchasing.
