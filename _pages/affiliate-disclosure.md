<p class="lede">How commercial links support this publication and how they are identified.</p>
<div class="note"><strong>Required Amazon disclosure:</strong><br>As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.</div>

      <p>Small Space Signal may include affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and make an eligible purchase, this site may receive a commission. You do not pay a higher price because of that commission.</p>

      <h2>How links are labeled</h2>

      <p>Articles containing affiliate links will include a clear disclosure near the relevant recommendation or link. Labels may include “affiliate link,” “paid link,” or “#CommissionsEarned.” Readers should not have to search for the disclosure.</p>

      <h2>Editorial independence</h2>

      <p>Compensation does not guarantee coverage or a favorable conclusion. Selection criteria are developed around reader needs such as fit, maintenance, noise, durability, and rental risk. A product may be excluded when it fails those criteria even if an affiliate relationship is available.</p>

      <p>Small Space Signal does not currently accept payment for positive reviews. If that changes, the sponsorship will be disclosed clearly in the relevant content.</p>

      <h2>Prices and availability</h2>

      <p>Retail prices and availability change frequently. Unless current information is supplied through an approved retailer tool, this site avoids presenting a price as guaranteed. Readers should verify the final price, seller, delivery date, and return terms on the retailer’s page before purchasing.</p>
