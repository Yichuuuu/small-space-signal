<p class="lede">A plain-language description of the limited data this site uses.</p>
<h2>Information collected by this site</h2>

<p>Small Space Signal is currently a static publication. It does not provide user accounts, accept comments, run an email list, or intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment information.</p>

      <h2>Basic hosting information</h2>

<p>The hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose reliability problems. The exact handling of that information depends on the hosting provider used for the public deployment.</p>

      <h2>Affiliate links</h2>

<p>If affiliate links are added, the destination retailer may use cookies or similar technology to attribute an eligible purchase to this site. Small Space Signal does not control the retailer’s systems. Review the destination retailer’s privacy policy before using its service.</p>

      <h2>Analytics and advertising</h2>

<p>No third-party analytics or personalized advertising scripts are included in the current version of this site. If either is added later, this policy will be updated to identify the provider and explain the relevant choices available to visitors.</p>

      <h2>Children’s privacy</h2>

<p>This publication is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>

      <h2>Policy changes</h2>

<p>This policy may be updated when site features or service providers change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.</p>
